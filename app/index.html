What the verdicts mean

  • All-time low — the lowest price the deal trackers have seen for this item.
  • Strong deal — a deep cut off list price, at or near the year’s best.
  • Bundle value — the saving comes from what is bundled in, not a price cut.
  • Modest cut — a real cut, but small against the list price.
  • Barely a deal — barely below list; only worth it if you wanted it anyway.
